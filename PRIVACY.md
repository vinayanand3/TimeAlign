# TimeAlign Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 28, 2026*

TimeAlign does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- **No data collection.** The app has no accounts, analytics, tracking, advertising, or crash
  reporting.
- **No network access.** TimeAlign never connects to the internet. The city list it searches is
  built into the app.
- **Your settings stay on your Mac.** The cities you add and your preferences (24-hour clock,
  working hours, window position) are saved only on your Mac, in the app's own storage. Deleting
  the app removes them.
- **Clipboard.** TimeAlign writes to the clipboard only when you choose a Copy action. It never
  reads the clipboard on its own.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/vinayanand3/TimeAlign/issues
